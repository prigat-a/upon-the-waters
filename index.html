Questions

Good to know

Is the water truly from the Sea of Galilee?

Yes. Every bottle is gathered at the Sea of Galilee or the Dead Sea and comes with a certificate of origin naming the place it was collected.

Is the water already blessed?

The water comes from the holy places themselves. Many customers bring it to their own priest or pastor to be blessed according to their tradition.

Can the water be drunk?

No. The waters are keepsakes for prayer, blessing and display. Dead Sea water is extremely salty; keep it away from the eyes and out of reach of children.

How is the Dead Sea mud used?

For centuries Dead Sea mud has been used to care for the face and skin. Apply a thin layer, rest for 10–15 minutes, then rinse with warm water. For external use only; test on a small area first.

How long does delivery take?

Orders leave the Holy Land within 3 business days and usually arrive within 7–14 days, depending on your country.

Do you serve churches and parishes?

Yes. Churches, parishes and gift shops receive special prices on orders of 20 packages or more. Write to us below.
