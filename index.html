The Holy Places

Where your gift comes from

The Sea of Galilee

The heart of Jesus' ministry. On these shores he called Peter, Andrew, James and John to be fishers of men. Here he calmed the storm, fed the multitude, and walked upon the water.

“Be of good cheer; it is I; be not afraid.”Matthew 14:27

Our water is gathered on the western shore, near Tiberias, Capernaum and the Mount of Beatitudes.

The Dead Sea

The lowest place on earth, where the Jordan River ends its journey. It lies near Jericho, near the wilderness where John preached, and near Qumran, where the Dead Sea Scrolls were found.

“These waters issue out toward the east country … and the waters shall be healed.”Ezekiel 47:8

The prophet Ezekiel saw a vision of this very sea made new and full of life.

Questions

Good to know

Is the water really from the Sea of Galilee?

Yes. Every bottle is gathered at the Sea of Galilee or the Dead Sea and comes with a certificate of origin stating where it was collected.

Can I drink the water?

No. The waters are keepsakes for prayer, blessing and display, and are not for drinking. Dead Sea water is extremely salty and should be kept away from the eyes and out of reach of children.

How is the Dead Sea mud used?

For centuries people have spread Dead Sea mud on the face and skin. Apply a thin layer, leave it for 10–15 minutes, then rinse with warm water. For external use only. Test on a small area first.

Can my priest or pastor bless the water?

Of course. Many customers bring their bottle to church to be blessed, or use it at a baptism, a wedding or a home blessing.

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship from Israel within 3 business days. Delivery usually takes 7–14 days, depending on your country.

Do you offer orders for churches and groups?

Yes. Churches, groups and gift shops get special prices on 20 packages or more. Use the form below to reach us.
