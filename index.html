The Story

Where the story happened, told again

Two thousand years ago, on a stormy night on this lake, the disciples saw a figure coming toward them across the water. Peter stepped out of the boat to meet him, and began to sink when his faith faltered.

Upon the Waters brings that night back to life on the Sea of Galilee itself, with actors, live music, fishing boats on the lake and the hills of Galilee behind them as the sun goes down.

Whether you sit on the shore in Tiberias or watch from your living room on another continent, you are part of the audience.

“But straightway Jesus spake unto them, saying, Be of good cheer; it is I; be not afraid.” Matthew 14:27

Questions

Good to know

Where exactly does the performance take place?

On the Tiberias waterfront, on the western shore of the Sea of Galilee. Exact meeting point and directions are emailed with your ticket.

What happens if the weather is bad?

If the performance is cancelled for weather, in-person guests can move to another date or get a full refund. The livestream is rescheduled automatically.

Is the show suitable for children?

Yes. The performance is about 50 minutes long and is suitable for all ages. Children under 6 sit on the shore free with an adult.

How long does shipping take?

Keepsakes ship from Israel within 3 business days. Delivery usually takes 7–14 days depending on your country.

Can churches or groups book together?

Yes. Groups of 15 or more get a discount and can reserve a private boat. Use the form below to get in touch.
